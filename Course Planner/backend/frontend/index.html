Your planner, your browser

Planner accounts and progress are stored locally in the browser for this deployment. They do not automatically sync to another device, so export a backup if you need one. This is an independent planning tool, not an official UIU system or a substitute for academic advising.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

Does this connect to my official UIU account?

No. Create a separate planner account. The app does not verify your UIU password or connect to university systems.

Will my plan sync across my devices?

No. This deployment stores planner data in the current browser. Use the export feature to save a copy, and take care before clearing browser data.

Are the course list and prerequisites official?

The planner includes a course list and prerequisite information for guidance, but it may not reflect every curriculum update or individual requirement. Confirm decisions with official UIU resources or an academic advisor.

How is CGPA calculated?

The planner computes a credit-weighted average from grades recorded for courses marked complete. It is a planning estimate based on the information you enter.
